Anatomy constraints (each on/off)
The watch sits on the forearm, so it reads the forearm directly. A small neural network trained on your own camera-labelled sessions estimates where the upper arm is, and the shoulder/elbow verdict follows from how the upper arm and forearm move. Slow movements are the hardest case. The facing direction drifts a little over minutes, so recalibrate now and then.
